# D5: Effective-dated rules selected by due date

**Status:** accepted

Rules live in `data/rules.json` with `effective_from`/`effective_to` and are
selected by the pay period's due date, never by "today". Transition periods
(May 2026: Res. 598/2022 to Res. 340/2026) are enumerated and always escalate
to a qualified specialist rather than being interpreted.

**Why.** UC-14 ("which rule applied to my May pay?") is the product's
sharpest correctness claim. Rule parameters need qualified sign-off before
any launch.
